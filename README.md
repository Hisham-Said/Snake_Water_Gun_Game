Snake Water Gun Game
Play the classic childhood game against the computer! Snake beats Water, Water beats Gun, and Gun beats Snake. Make your choice and see if you can win. Simple, fun.
Snake drinks Water → Snake Wins
Water rusts Gun → Water Wins
Gun shoots Snake → Gun Wins
Same choice = Tie
How to Play
Run the program:
Enter your choice: snake, water, or gun
Computer makes its choice randomly
See who wins!
Example
Enter your choice : snake
You Choose : snake
Computer choose : water
Snake Beats Water.
You Win!
Features:
Simple text-based gameplay
Random computer choices
Easy to understand rules
Quick and fun!
